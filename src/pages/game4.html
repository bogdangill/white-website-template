About game:

Explore a variety of places. From settings, to lively areas. As you witness the difficulty level increase gradually over time.Enjoy an environment where imagination combines with reason to test your ability, for thinking and keen observation.

Game Features:

Genres: Action, shooter.

Platform: Online (browser).

Age rating: 16+.

Game modes: Single player and cooperative mode.

Graphics: Detailed 3D animation with gloomy post-apocalyptic landscapes.

Levels: A variety of levels, from city streets to abandoned factories and forests.

Characters: Several characters with unique skills and stories.

Weapons: A wide variety of weapons and ammunition that can be upgraded.
